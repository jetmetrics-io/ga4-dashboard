JetMetrics Funnel Dashboard Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains what JetMetrics Funnel Dashboard (the dashboard at jetmetrics.io/funnel-dashboard) accesses, how it uses that information and where it is kept. JetMetrics (“we”) runs the dashboard. This policy covers the dashboard only; the rest of jetmetrics.io is covered by the website privacy policy. Questions: mary@jetmetrics.io.

In short: the dashboard reads your Google Analytics data only to show it to you. Reports go from Google straight to your browser. Our sign-in service keeps you signed in and checks that your email address has access to the dashboard; it stores nothing. We don't sell your Google data or share it beyond that access check.

What the dashboard accesses

When you click “Connect Google Analytics”, Google asks you to allow:

  • See and download your Google Analytics data (read-only). The dashboard uses it to list the GA4 properties your Google account can open and to request reports for the property, period and filters you choose.
  • See your primary Google Account email address. Our sign-in service uses it to check that this address has access to the dashboard (see “Access check”), and the dashboard uses it to pre-select your account the next time you connect.

The dashboard can't change anything in your Google Analytics account.

How the information is used

  • Reports from the Google Analytics Data API (sessions, funnel events, revenue and the breakdowns shown on the dashboard) are used to calculate and display the dashboard to you, in your browser.
  • When you click “Copy for Claude” or “Download”, the dashboard's numbers are copied to your clipboard or saved as a file on your device. Where you paste or send them is up to you; the dashboard doesn't send them anywhere.
  • We don't use Google data for advertising, don't sell it and don't use it to train AI models. No person at JetMetrics can see it, because it never reaches us.

Where it is stored

Only in your browser, on your device:

  • the access token from Google, which expires after one hour;
  • a sign-in session: your Google refresh token, encrypted so that only our sign-in service can read it. It lets the dashboard get a new access token every hour without asking you to sign in again;
  • your email address (for pre-selecting your account);
  • your choices: the selected property, the period and comparison, and the Driver Tree targets for each property.

Reports stay in the page's memory while the page is open and are gone when you close it.

Our sign-in service at api.jetmetrics.io exchanges Google's one-time sign-in code for tokens and renews the access token from your sign-in session. It also checks your access with Gumroad (see “Access check”). It returns the tokens to your browser and keeps nothing: no tokens, no email address and no Analytics data. Google Analytics reports never pass through it.

Access check

You get access to the dashboard on our store at Gumroad, with your email address. When you connect, our sign-in service sends the email address of your Google account to Gumroad to look up whether it has access. Gumroad already has this address if you signed up with it there. Only the address is sent; your Google Analytics data never is. If the address isn't found, the dashboard shows where to get access, and you can connect another Google account instead.

Sharing

We don't sell Google user data and don't share or transfer it to anyone, except for sending your email address to Gumroad for the access check above.

JetMetrics Funnel Dashboard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Services that deliver the page

  • Google: sign-in and the Google Analytics APIs. Analytics requests go directly from your browser to Google.
  • Cloudflare Workers runs our sign-in service (see “Where it is stored”).
  • Gumroad runs our store and receives your email address for the access check (see “Access check”). Its privacy policy: gumroad.com/privacy.
  • Tilda hosts the page, Cloudflare delivers the jetmetrics.io website, and jsDelivr serves the dashboard's code. Like any website, they receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Visits to jetmetrics.io pages, including this one and the dashboard page, are measured with Google Analytics (pages viewed, device, approximate location). This is about visits to our website and doesn't include your Google Analytics data.

Your control

  • Click “Disconnect” in the dashboard: it revokes the dashboard's access in your Google account and removes the session, the token and your email address from this browser.
  • Remove the dashboard's access at any time in your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything the dashboard stored on your device.

Children

The dashboard is a business tool and is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If the dashboard starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
