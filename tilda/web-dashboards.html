How it works

  1. Open a dashboard and click “Connect Google Analytics”.
  2. In Google's window, allow JetMetrics Web Dashboards read-only access to your Google Analytics data.
  3. Pick your GA4 property. The dashboard loads with your numbers and keeps you signed in.

Your data

JetMetrics Web Dashboards ask Google for read-only access to your Google Analytics data and for your email address. They use them only to list your GA4 properties, build the dashboard you open and check your access. Reports go from Google straight to your browser and are shown only to you: JetMetrics doesn't receive, store or share your Analytics data. You can disconnect at any time.
